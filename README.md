# My Favorite Movie

## Hacksaw Ridge

**Genre:** Action, War, Drama

### Description
Hacksaw Ridge is one of the best war movies, especially because Andrew Garfield stars as Desmond Doss. The movie tells an inspiring story of courage, bravery, and determination.

### Why I Like It
- Andrew Garfield is an amazing actor.
- The movie has an interesting and emotional storyline.
- It is based on a true story.
- It shows the importance of courage and helping others.
- Desmond Doss inspires me because he stays true to his beliefs.

### My Favorite Character
**Desmond Doss** – I admire his bravery, determination, and willingness to save others despite the challenges he faces.

### Rating
**10/10**
